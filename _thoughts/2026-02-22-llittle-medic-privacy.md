**LittleMedic Privacy Policy**

*Effective date: November 1, 2026 · Version 1.3*

**1. Who We Are**

LittleMedic is developed by Stipe Grbić, who is responsible for the processing of personal data described in this policy (the data controller). The app is designed to help parents and caregivers track children's health information, including illnesses, medications, and medical appointments.

**2. Data We Collect**

*Local storage (all users)*

The app stores on your device:

- Children's names, birthdates, profile information, and profile photos
- Illness records (symptoms, dates, notes, and any photos you attach)
- Medication records (name, dosage, schedule, linked illnesses)
- Medical appointment records (type, date, time, clinic, doctor, notes)
- Medication administration logs

This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

*iCloud Sync (iOS, Premium, optional)*

When enabled, the above data, including child profile photos and illness images, is backed up to your personal iCloud account. The developer has no access to your iCloud data.

*Family Sync (optional; creating a group requires Premium)*

When you create or join a Family Sync group, the above health data (excluding photos) is transmitted to and stored on Supabase-hosted servers (hosted on AWS infrastructure in Ireland, EU) so it can be shared with the other members of your group. The following applies:

- Data is only uploaded after you create or join a group
- Data is shared only with the members of your family group
- Family Sync uses an anonymous sign-in: no email address or real name is required, only a group name and an invite code
- Supabase acts as our hosting provider (data processor), and its privacy policy applies to the infrastructure layer: [supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy)

**3. Data We Do NOT Collect**

We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics, and we do not use advertising SDKs or tracking libraries or collect your advertising identifier. Sign-in is not required for core functionality. The app does not access contacts, microphone, or location, and uses the camera and photo library only when you choose to attach a photo.

**4. Notifications**

Notifications are scheduled locally via the operating system's APIs. No data is sent to a server to deliver them.

**5. Data Export and Import**

You control when backup files are created and where they are stored. The app does not upload anything automatically unless you turn on iCloud Sync or Family Sync.

**6. Children's Privacy**

The app is meant for parents and caregivers, not children. We do not sell or share data about children with third parties. Family Sync data is shared only with the other members of the family group you create or join, and is stored by our hosting provider solely to provide the service.

**7. Data Security**

Local data is protected by your device's security (passcode and device encryption). Family Sync data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest on Supabase's servers, and access is restricted to the members of your family group. As the operator of the service, the developer could technically access data stored for Family Sync, but does not view, use, or share it except where necessary to keep the service running or when required by law.

**8. Data Retention and Deletion**

- You can delete individual records at any time. Deleting a child profile removes all associated data.
- Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data stored on your device.
- Family Sync data is kept on the server for as long as the family group exists. Records you delete in the app are also removed from the server.
- If you created a group, **Family Sync → Delete family group** removes the group and all of its synced data from the server.
- If you joined a group, **Family Sync → Leave family group** removes you from the group. Records you added stay available to the other members because they are shared family records. Ask the group owner to delete the group, or contact us, to have them removed.
- Once you are no longer in a group, **Family Sync → Remove my data from Family Sync** deletes your Family Sync account, along with any remaining groups you own, from the server.
- Uninstalling the app does not delete Family Sync data from the server, so delete or leave your group first, or contact us.
- Deleted data may remain in our hosting provider's routine backups for a limited period before it is overwritten.
- iCloud Sync data can be deleted at any time from your device's iCloud storage settings.

**9. Your Rights**

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, export, or delete the Family Sync data stored about you, or to restrict or object to its processing. Email us at the address below and we will respond within one month.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process Family Sync data on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give by creating or joining a group. You can withdraw it at any time by leaving or deleting your group or by contacting us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

**10. Third-Party Services**

- **Supabase** (Family Sync only): cloud database infrastructure. Data is stored only when you create or join a Family Sync group.
- **Apple iCloud** (iCloud Sync only): personal cloud backup, governed by Apple's privacy policy.
- **Apple App Store / Google Play**: purchases and subscriptions are processed by the app stores, and we do not receive your payment details. App stores may collect download and crash data independently of this app.

**11. Changes to This Policy**

Updates will be reflected with a new effective date published at the same URL.
