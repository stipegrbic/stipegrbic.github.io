Quiet Signal — Support

Quiet Signal is a private, on-device habit and health journal — track what matters to you, see how
often it actually happens, and get honest, data-grounded insight without an account or any
company watching along.

Need help, found a bug, or have feedback?

Common questions

How do I delete my data?
Everything you log is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it — there's no
account and nothing stored on a server.

Does my data sync across devices?
No. Quiet Signal has no account and no cloud sync — your data stays on the one device you log it
on.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?
Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. Go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your
iPhone to view, change, or cancel at any time.

Is my data private?
Yes. There's no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising. Full details: [link to your hosted
privacy policy — the one already in AppLinks.swift: https://stipe.xyz/thoughts/2026-09-26-quiet-signal-privacy.html]

Why don't I see a correlation yet?
Quiet Signal only shows a pattern once there's genuinely enough logged data to trust it — this
takes a little time and keeps every insight honest rather than guessed at.
