Jobline Support

Have a question or need help with Jobline? We're happy to assist.

Email: [stipegrbic@hotmail.com](mailto:stipegrbic@hotmail.com)

We typically respond within 1–2 business days.

Common topics:

- Restoring a Jobline Pro purchase — Settings > Subscription > Restore purchases

- Setting up cloud backup — Settings > Back up my data, then enter that code on your other device under Settings > Restore from a backup

- Adding another trade — Settings > Add a trade

- Exporting your data — Settings > Export jobs & invoices (CSV)

- Cancelling your subscription — manage it directly in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions (or Google Play > Subscriptions on Android)

- Deleting your data — Settings > Delete all my data
